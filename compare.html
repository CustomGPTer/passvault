What PassVault does not do

PassVault has no syncing between devices, no sharing with other people, no passkeys, no browser extension and no breach alerts, because it never goes online. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Bitwarden and 1Password sync your vault across phones, computers and browsers, share logins with family or colleagues, and store passkeys. Bitwarden publishes yearly independent security audits, 1Password warns you when an account turns up in a data breach, and both update themselves from their app stores.
